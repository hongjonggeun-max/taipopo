Data Deletion Guide

TAIPOPO

Last updated: 2026-11-23

1. App Account

TAIPOPO does not create or operate a separate app account.

2. Data Stored Inside the App

Workplace information, worker information, attendance and work records, photos, settlements, reports, PDFs, app settings, attachments, and export/backup-related files are stored primarily in the app storage area on the device. Based on the currently confirmed implementation, the app is not structured to upload and store the user's work records on its own server.

3. Deleting Data Inside the App

Records can be deleted individually in each feature. Photos can be moved to the trash, restored, or permanently deleted. App-managed data stored on the device can be deleted from the following path.

Settings > Data Management > Reset All Data

Reset All Data deletes app settings, work data, photos inside the app, PDFs created inside the app, attachments inside the app, export/backup data inside the app, and cache/temp data. The app may close after deletion is complete, and if there is no separately kept backup, deleted device app data cannot be recovered.

4. App Deletion

When the app is deleted, data in the app storage area on the device may be removed according to operating system policies. However, this does not mean that files exported or copied outside the app, or copies kept in other apps or storage devices are all deleted. Before deleting the app, back up needed materials or organize external copies yourself.

5. Backup ZIPs and external copies

This review candidate does not provide Google account linking or direct Drive API backup, restore, or automatic upload resumption. TAIPOPO creates the backup ZIP on your device. When you export it, the files are passed to the app you select in the share sheet. Manage or delete copies stored outside TAIPOPO at the location where you saved them. After creating the backup ZIP, choose an app in the Android share sheet to keep it. Check the save status in the app you chose. Keep all files of a split backup together. Choose the backup ZIP on your device or in storage shown by the file picker. A split backup requires every file from the same backup. Older Google Drive copies: if an earlier version created an appDataFolder backup, it is an external copy separate from the current version's ZIP. Resetting or uninstalling the app does not delete that copy. This version does not directly back up, restore or manage it. See Google's official instructions for managing or deleting it in the Google account that owns it. https://support.google.com/drive/answer/6374270

6. Files Outside the App

Files exported to Downloads, files shared to other apps, files sent by email, files copied to the cloud, USB or external storage devices, or ZIP/PDF/photo files separately kept by the user may remain separately from deletion of data inside the app. Those copies must be deleted separately from the stored location or receiving service.

7. Firebase Crashlytics Diagnostic Information

Resetting the app’s local data or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete diagnostic information already sent to Firebase Crashlytics.

This app does not set a custom Crashlytics user ID. Firebase Support confirmed that, with this setup, historical reports for an individual user cannot be located or deleted using an FID or Crashlytics installation identifier. Deleting the FID does not delete reports already sent. This limitation does not apply to deleting support email or work records on the device.

8. Back Up Before Deletion

Save work records, settings, photos, and other data on your device as a backup ZIP. After creating the backup ZIP, choose an app in the Android share sheet to keep it. Check the save status in the app you chose. Keep all files of a split backup together. Running restore may replace the current device data with the backup contents.

9. Deletion Procedure and Contact

Because the app does not have a separate user account or work data stored on its own server, device app data can be deleted using Reset All Data above.

Contact: taipopo.app@gmail.com

The operator uses only information needed to handle the inquiry. Once the inquiry and necessary follow-up are complete and the processing purpose has been fulfilled, inquiry information and attachments are deleted without delay. Received and reply emails are permanently deleted from the account, and copies managed by the operator, including downloads, are also deleted. Only records actually subject to a statutory retention duty are stored separately from other inquiries for the legally required period.

Send deletion requests to taipopo.app@gmail.com. Requests are handled after verifying your identity or an authorized representative's authority using the minimum information necessary. Records that must be retained by law are excluded from deletion only to the extent that the duty applies, and the legal basis and retention period are explained. The Gmail provider's removal of information from its servers and backups is separate from deletion of copies managed by the operator.

The operator uses Gmail to receive, store and reply to inquiries. This policy governs the operator’s handling and deletion of inquiries. The sender’s chosen email service also participates in delivery, and each email service’s own processing is also subject to its policies.

Support inquiries necessary to perform the app-use contract or take steps you request when entering into a contract are processed under Article 15(1)(4) of South Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act. Personal-data rights requests are processed under Article 15(1)(2) and the applicable provisions of Articles 35–38. Only the sender email address, inquiry and related material needed for each request are processed; these grounds are not applied indiscriminately to general suggestions or unnecessary attachments.

Privacy choices are stored separately on this device and excluded from ZIP. A full data reset returns them to the initial state. Withdrawing diagnostics and resetting the app do not perform the same deletion of sent diagnostics or externally shared copies. A request to delete unsent reports does not confirm deletion of all SDK queues. Contact us to request deletion of information already sent; the actual scope must be checked against the service and applicable law.
