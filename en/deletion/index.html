Data Deletion Guide

Effective date: to be confirmed before the official release

1. App Account

The reviewed app has no separate TAIPOPO registration process or app password.

The selected Google account is used for Google connection, Drive backup, restore and backup management when authentication is needed. Drive backups use appDataFolder, not access to all Drive files.

2. Data Stored Inside the App

Workplace information, worker information, attendance and work records, photos, settlements, reports, PDFs, app settings, attachments, and export/backup-related files are stored primarily in the app storage area on the device. Based on the currently confirmed implementation, the app is not structured to upload and store the user's work records on its own server.

3. Deleting Data Inside the App

Records can be deleted individually in each feature. Photos can be moved to the trash, restored, or permanently deleted. App-managed data stored on the device can be deleted from the following path.

Settings > Backup & Restore > Data Management > Reset All Data

Reset All Data deletes app settings, work data, photos inside the app, PDFs created inside the app, attachments inside the app, export/backup data inside the app, and cache/temp data. The app may close after deletion is complete, and if there is no separately kept backup, deleted device app data cannot be recovered.

4. App Deletion

When the app is deleted, data in the app storage area on the device may be removed according to operating system policies. However, this does not mean that files exported or copied outside the app, Google Drive backups, or copies kept in other apps or storage devices are all deleted. Before deleting the app, back up needed materials or organize external copies yourself.

5. Google Drive Backups

Google Drive backups directly created by the user are stored in Google Drive's app-specific appDataFolder. Backups are created only when the user directly runs the feature and are not automatic uploads. Local Reset All Data and disconnecting the Google account do not automatically delete Drive backups that have already been stored. Completed backups are deleted only when the user selects a specific backup in Google Drive backup management and confirms deletion.

6. Files Outside the App

Files exported to Downloads, files shared to other apps, files sent by email, files copied to the cloud, USB or external storage devices, or ZIP/PDF/photo files separately kept by the user may remain separately from deletion of data inside the app. Those copies must be deleted separately from the stored location or receiving service.

7. Firebase Crashlytics Diagnostic Information

Resetting the app’s local data or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete diagnostic information already sent to Firebase Crashlytics.

8. Back Up Before Deletion

Before changing devices, deleting the app, or resetting all data, the user can directly back up needed records to Google Drive from Settings > Backup & Restore > Data Backup. Running restore may replace the current device data with the backup contents.

9. Deletion Procedure and Contact

Because the app does not have a separate user account or work data stored on its own server, device app data can be deleted using Reset All Data above. If you need help with the deletion procedure or Google Drive backups, contact the email below.

Contact: taipopo.app@gmail.com

Diagnostic choices and deletion · review draft 2026-11-07

Privacy choices are stored separately on this device and excluded from ZIP and Drive backups. A full data reset returns them to the initial state. Withdrawing diagnostics, resetting the app and disconnecting Google do not perform the same deletion of sent diagnostics, Drive backups or externally shared copies. A request to delete unsent reports does not confirm deletion of all SDK queues. Contact us to request deletion of information already sent; the actual scope must be checked against the service and applicable law.

External processing, deletion and app versions · 2026-11-08

Withdrawing diagnostics, resetting local data, disconnecting Google, deleting Drive backups, deleting external copies and deleting transmitted diagnostics are different procedures. Contact taipopo.app@gmail.com about transmitted data or received email. Unverified deletion completion or retention periods are not promised.
