3. Purposes and Basis for Processing

The information described above comes from what you enter, capture or attach in the app, or from your direct use of its features. Information is used to provide attendance and work record management, photo records storage, settlement, report and PDF creation, backup and restore, notifications, error diagnostics, and inquiry handling.

4. Retention

Work data and photos may remain in the app's storage until you delete individual items, permanently delete photos, reset all data, uninstall the app, or clear its data through the operating system. The trash rules and exceptions in section 10 also apply. Reset All Data deletes settings, work data, photos, generated PDFs, attachments, exported files, backup data, caches, and temporary files within the app-managed area on the device. Copies stored outside the app-managed area must be deleted separately. You must delete exported ZIP/PDF/photo files and copies on external storage separately at their saved locations. Diagnostic data sent to Firebase Crashlytics is handled according to Firebase's features and policies.

Device loss or failure, uninstalling the app, or clearing app data can cause loss of records and photos. Backups stored only on the same device can also be lost; keep important backups outside the device too. You need to check and manage backup completion, location, file condition, and account access. The developer keeps no record copies on a separate server and may be unable to help recover data without a valid backup. See the relevant privacy policy sections for external service processing such as diagnostics.

5. Backup ZIPs and external copies

TAIPOPO creates the backup ZIP on your device. When you export it, the files are passed to the app you select in the share sheet. Manage or delete copies stored outside TAIPOPO at the location where you saved them.

This standard ZIP is an unencrypted copy of the work data, settings, photos and other data included in the backup; it does not include Terms confirmation or acceptance records. It can include original photos and their location, capture-time, and device metadata, along with workplaces, addresses, work records, pay, and notes. Saving or sharing creates a copy outside the app that the selected person or app can open.

Version 1.0.0(17) does not provide Google account connection, direct Drive API backup or restore, or automatic upload resumption. Backup ZIPs are created on your device and can be saved on the device or external storage. When exporting an external backup, choose the receiving app in the Android share sheet. To restore, select a backup from the device backup list or the file picker. Drive appDataFolder backups made by older versions are separate from ordinary ZIPs saved by sharing and are not automatically deleted by an update or local reset. This version cannot directly list or delete those older backups. Contact support if you need help.

6. Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics diagnostics are off by default. With your consent, error codes, sanitized call traces, installation identifiers processed by the SDK, and device/app diagnostic information may be collected, used, and sent to Google Firebase to investigate app errors and improve stability. Under South Korean law, collection and use are based on consent under Article 15(1)(1) of the Personal Information Protection Act. Work records, photos, and inquiry text are not sent as diagnostic content. You can use records, photography, and backups without consenting.

Withdrawal blocks new error reports from the app. SDK traffic already in progress cannot be recalled. Before Firebase restarts after withdrawal or this update’s migration, retained Crashlytics reports and queued Firebase session diagnostics are removed; cleanup failure keeps Firebase error reporting off. Allowing again in the same app process waits for a full process restart and cleanup, preventing retroactive submission of OFF-period reports. The current setup does not support looking up or deleting past individual Crashlytics reports for a specific user. Deleting a Firebase installation ID (FID) does not delete past Crashlytics reports that have already been sent. This choice does not control ML Kit diagnostics. Retained ML Kit technical diagnostics may be sent separately.

Crashlytics diagnostic information is separate from the user's work records, photos and settlement data. Resetting local app data or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete diagnostics already sent to Firebase Crashlytics. For inquiries sent to taipopo.app@gmail.com, we explain the measures currently available and the provider's limitations separately.

7. QR recognition

Versions containing ML Kit may process technical diagnostics externally, including SDK installation identifiers, device, app and event information, even when QR images are decoded on the device. Firebase diagnostic choices do not control ML Kit processing.

Versions that use ZXing process QR images on the device and do not use ML Kit for QR scanning. Updating the app does not automatically delete earlier diagnostic queues or data already sent to servers. Remaining diagnostic data may be transmitted separately.

9. Sharing and Exporting Photos, PDFs and ZIP Files

Photos, PDFs, ZIP files, and other export files may be copied to an external app, storage location, cloud, USB, or external storage device selected by the user only when the user directly chooses to share, save, or export them. Retention and deletion of copies transferred outside the app-managed area may vary depending on the app, storage location, service, and user settings.

10. Deletion and Your Rights

Items moved to the trash, including photos, memos, workplaces, settlements, and reports, can be restored or permanently deleted. Items that have been in the trash for 30 days from the deletion date are permanently deleted the next time the trash is opened and cannot be restored from the app's trash after permanent deletion. A user can also permanently delete an item before 30 days.

Legacy items without deletion-date information are excluded from time-based automatic deletion. Photos still used by other records are protected during deletion. Automatic cleanup may be deferred while data is being restored or records cannot be loaded normally.

Users can view, edit, and delete records in the app, and photos can be restored from the trash or permanently deleted. Reset All Data deletes settings, work data, photos inside the app, PDFs created inside the app, attachments, export/backup data inside the app, and cache/temp data in the app-managed area on the device. App-managed data on the device can be deleted through Settings > Data Management > Reset All Data.

Files exported to Downloads, ZIP/PDF/photo files shared or copied to another app, email, cloud, USB, or external storage device, and diagnostic information already sent to Crashlytics are separate from local reset. Privacy inquiries and app data deletion procedure questions can be sent to taipopo.app@gmail.com.

Requests for access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, guidance on the app data deletion procedure, or exercise of rights by a statutory representative can be sent to taipopo.app@gmail.com. While handling a request, the minimum information needed to verify the requester’s identity or legitimate authority may be requested.

11. Security and Automatic Data Collection

The app primarily manages work data and photos in its storage area on your device. Based on the confirmed app structure, TAIPOPO does not use its own web cookies or similar files to automatically collect your work data.

The support mailbox uses two-step verification. Its current settings have no delegated mail users or forwarding addresses, and POP download and Gmail Offline mail storage are disabled. These settings do not automatically protect or delete manually downloaded or separately stored files. Retention and deletion of inquiries and external copies are described in their respective sections.

13. Website visits and theme storage

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security. This site has no sign-up, advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Hosting processing and retention are described in the GitHub Privacy Statement: GitHub

Selecting light mode changes the saved value; it does not remove it. The site works without storage; if no saved choice can be read, it defaults to light mode.

Received mail is handled through Gmail and your chosen email service under their policies. For questions about retention or deletion of received mail, contact taipopo.app@gmail.com. 15. Rights under applicable law

Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. Send requests to taipopo.app@gmail.com; only information necessary to verify identity or authority may be requested. 16. Permissions and processing grounds

Android permissions enable particular functions; they do not by themselves establish a legal basis or blanket consent for all personal-data processing. Permission withdrawal, deletion of local records and deletion of external copies are separate actions. External services' processing and retention follow the relevant service policies.

This notice applies to versions with automatic address lookup blocked. Earlier installations may still perform address lookups and remain subject to their version's notices and settings. Updating alone does not delete existing records or information already sent outside the device.

17. Terms acceptance records

The current Play internal-test +17 provides Terms viewing only. The new acceptance step and local records have been added to a separate validation build and have not been distributed.

To check which Terms apply, the accepted Terms version, acceptance decision and time, and privacy notice version are stored in this device's app settings. Launching the app or reading a document is not recorded as acceptance. The operator does not receive these records on a server, and they are not included in diagnostics or backup ZIPs.

Earlier acceptance records are kept on the device while app data is retained. Ordinary wording changes are distinguished from changes requiring new acceptance. Resetting all app data or uninstalling the app may remove these device records. The operator has no feature for remotely reading or deleting records held only on your device.

Formal acceptance is recorded only after the applicable document and effective date are finalized; other confirmations to proceed are not treated as formal acceptance of the Terms.
