TAIPOPO Privacy Policy

Read the app privacy policy in English below. The policy explains the information processed, storage, external services, retention, deletion and contact details.

Last updated: 2026-11-07

Data controller and privacy officer: independent developer Jonggeun Hong

This web notice distinguishes versions containing ML Kit from local validation candidate 1.0.0+21. Direct device review is pending; the candidate description does not identify versions distributed on Google Play.

Data controller and privacy officer: independent developer Jonggeun Hong

taipopo.app@gmail.com

2. Information Processed

TAIPOPO stores information needed for attendance, workplaces, photo records, settlements, and report management primarily in the app storage area on the user's device. Based on the currently confirmed implementation, TAIPOPO is not structured to upload and store the user's work data on its own server.

  • User and worker information: worker name, phone number, email, address and detailed address, wage calculation criteria, payment date, payment method, bank, account number, and account holder
  • Workplace information: workplace name, address and detailed address, company name, manager name, contact information, workplace memos, and workplace attachments
  • Attendance and work records: check-in and check-out times, work and break time, work memos, settlement data, report data, and related snapshots
  • Photo and image information: includes original photos, edited photos, display images, thumbnails and other derived images, user-designated photo categories, markings and annotations added to photos, capture time, and workplace or work information linked to photos
  • Location and address information: GPS coordinates and address information when the user grants location permission and uses the related features. To convert coordinates into an address, the app calls the Android operating system’s geocoding service; depending on the device and operating-system configuration, this process may use the network.
  • Photo metadata: original photo files may contain capture-related metadata (additional photo information), such as EXIF. The app may read photo orientation information or apply it to derived images to display and share photos and generate PDFs.
  • Settings and diagnostics: app settings, notification, camera and backup settings, backup and restore progress information, and app stability and error diagnostic information

3. Purposes and Basis for Processing

The information described above comes from what you enter, capture or attach in the app, or from your direct use of its features. Information is used to provide attendance and work record management, photo records storage, settlement, report and PDF creation, backup and restore, notifications, error diagnostics, and inquiry handling. Location, camera, notifications, storage, Google Sign-In, and Google Drive are used when the relevant Android permission, Google OAuth consent, or direct user action is provided.

4. Retention

Work data and photos may remain in the app's storage until you delete individual items, permanently delete photos, reset all data, uninstall the app, or clear its data through the operating system. The trash rules and exceptions in section 10 also apply. Reset All Data deletes settings, work data, photos, generated PDFs, attachments, exported files, backup data, caches, and temporary files within the app-managed area on the device. Copies stored outside the app-managed area must be deleted separately. Google Drive backups may remain in appDataFolder until you delete them in Google Drive backup management. You must delete exported ZIP/PDF/photo files and copies on external storage separately at their saved locations. Diagnostic data sent to Firebase Crashlytics is handled according to Firebase's features and policies.

5. Google Drive and Google Sign-In

The selected Google account is used for Google connection, Drive backup, restore and backup management when authentication is needed. Drive backups use appDataFolder, not access to all Drive files.

A Google Drive backup may consist of a single ZIP file or a backup split across multiple files, and may include photos, location and address information, work records, settlements, reports, settings, and app data. The app does not automatically upload work data to Drive unless the user selects the action. Local data reset or disconnecting Google does not automatically delete existing Drive backups. Completed backups remain until the user selects a specific backup in Google Drive backup management and confirms deletion. Google’s policies may also apply to data processed by its services.

App-level encryption has not been confirmed for backup ZIP files or Drive backups. Users are therefore responsible for keeping backup files in encrypted storage or a trusted account.

6. Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics diagnostics are off by default. If you allow them, error codes, sanitized call traces, and SDK installation identifiers, device and app diagnostics may be sent to Google Firebase. The app does not send work records, photos or inquiry text as diagnostic content. You can use records, camera and backups without allowing diagnostics.

Withdrawal blocks new error reports from the app. SDK traffic already in progress cannot be recalled. Before Firebase restarts after withdrawal or this update’s migration, retained Crashlytics reports and queued Firebase session diagnostics are removed; cleanup failure keeps Firebase error reporting off. Allowing again in the same app process waits for a full process restart and cleanup, preventing retroactive submission of OFF-period reports. Deleting information already sent to the server requires a separate request. This choice does not control ML Kit diagnostics. Retained ML Kit technical diagnostics may be sent separately.

Crashlytics diagnostic information is separate from the user's work records, photos, and settlement data. Resetting the app’s local data or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete diagnostic information already sent to Firebase Crashlytics. Related inquiries can be sent to the email below, and the actual handling scope may vary depending on Firebase features and policies.

According to Firebase’s official guidance, Crashlytics retains call traces from the time of a crash and related identifiers for 90 days, then begins the process of deleting them from live and backup systems.

7. QR recognition

Versions containing ML Kit may process technical diagnostics externally, including SDK installation identifiers, device, app and event information, even when QR images are decoded on the device. Firebase diagnostic choices do not control ML Kit processing.

Local validation candidate 1.0.0+21 includes an on-device ZXing implementation and removes the ML Kit dependency. This does not mean real-device QR review is complete. Replacement does not delete earlier diagnostic queues or server data; remaining data may still be transmitted separately.

8. Third Parties and External Processing

Based on the confirmed implementation, TAIPOPO does not centrally store your work data on its own server or sell it. However, Google, Firebase, external apps, cloud services, or storage locations and USB or other external storage devices you select may process data when you start Google Drive backup or restore, Google Sign-In, sharing, export or external saving, or when the included Google, Firebase or ML Kit (previous versions) SDKs operate. Each service's terms and privacy policy may also apply.

9. Sharing and Exporting Photos, PDFs and ZIP Files

Photos, PDFs, ZIP files, and other export files may be copied to an external app, storage location, cloud, USB, or external storage device selected by the user only when the user directly chooses to share, save, or export them. Retention and deletion of copies transferred outside the app-managed area may vary depending on the app, storage location, service, and user settings.

10. Deletion and Your Rights

Items moved to the trash, including photos, memos, workplaces, settlements, and reports, can be restored or permanently deleted. Items that have been in the trash for 30 days from the deletion date are permanently deleted the next time the trash is opened and cannot be restored from the app's trash after permanent deletion. A user can also permanently delete an item before 30 days.

Legacy items without deletion-date information are excluded from time-based automatic deletion. Photos still used by other records are protected during deletion. Automatic cleanup may be deferred while data is being restored or records cannot be loaded normally.

Users can view, edit, and delete records in the app, and photos can be restored from the trash or permanently deleted. Reset All Data deletes settings, work data, photos inside the app, PDFs created inside the app, attachments, export/backup data inside the app, and cache/temp data in the app-managed area on the device. App-managed data on the device can be deleted through Settings > Backup & Restore > Data Management > Reset All Data.

Files exported to Downloads, ZIP/PDF/photo files shared or copied to another app, email, cloud, USB, or external storage device, Google Drive backups, and diagnostic information already sent to Crashlytics are separate from local reset. Privacy inquiries and app data deletion procedure questions can be sent to taipopo.app@gmail.com.

Requests for access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, guidance on the app data deletion procedure, or exercise of rights by a statutory representative can be sent to taipopo.app@gmail.com. While handling a request, the minimum information needed to verify the requester’s identity or legitimate authority may be requested.

11. Security and Automatic Data Collection

The app primarily manages work data and photos in its storage area on your device. Transfers you initiate, such as Google Drive backup or restore, sharing and export, use the transmission and storage security provided by the operating system, Google or the selected service. Based on the confirmed app structure, TAIPOPO does not use its own web cookies or similar files to automatically collect your work data.

12. Children and Policy Changes

TAIPOPO is not designed primarily for children under the age of 14. This policy may be revised according to changes in laws, services, or features, and changes will be announced through an app update or in-app notice.

13. Website visits and theme storage · 2026-11-07

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security. This site has no sign-up, advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Hosting processing and retention are described in the GitHub Privacy Statement: GitHub

The browser stores only the light/dark theme choice under taipopo-site-theme. Clear this site's browser data to remove it, or block site storage in your browser where supported to refuse persistence. Selecting light mode changes the saved value; it does not remove it. The site works without storage; if no saved choice can be read, it defaults to light mode.

14. Email inquiries

The website opens your email app and does not itself receive or store inquiries. If you send an email, the developer receives and processes the sender address, message, attachments and any device details you send to respond, troubleshoot or handle a rights request. App email drafts can include app/build version, Android version, device model and language; review and remove unnecessary details before sending. Received mail is handled through Gmail and your chosen email service under their policies. For questions about retention or deletion of received mail, contact taipopo.app@gmail.com. 15. Rights under applicable law

Where applicable law provides them, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, objection or data portability, and withdraw consent for processing based on consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. Send requests to taipopo.app@gmail.com; only information necessary to verify identity or authority may be requested. You may also complain to the competent data protection authority. EEA authority directory: EDPB

You manage device-only records in the app. The developer does not receive those records merely because you use the app and cannot remotely delete them. Data you send by email or through external services must be considered separately.

16. Permissions and processing grounds

Android permissions and Google OAuth authorization enable particular functions; they do not by themselves establish a legal basis or blanket consent for all personal-data processing. Permission withdrawal, Google disconnection, deletion of local records and deletion of external copies are separate actions. External services' processing and retention follow the relevant service policies.

External processing, deletion and app versions · 2026-11-08

The actual recipient entities, processing countries, retention and applicable transfer grounds for Google Sign-In/Drive, Firebase, support email and website hosting also require separate confirmation against the services and contracts in use.

Withdrawing diagnostics, resetting local data, disconnecting Google, deleting Drive backups, deleting external copies and deleting transmitted diagnostics are different procedures.
